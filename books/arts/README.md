# Arts 🎨

This folder contains books about visual arts, music, design, and creative expression.

## Topics Include:

- **Visual Arts**: Painting, sculpture, drawing, art history
- **Music**: Music theory, composition, music history, instruments
- **Design**: Graphic design, UI/UX design, industrial design
- **Photography**: Techniques, composition, photo editing
- **Architecture**: Architectural design, history of architecture
- **Film & Cinema**: Filmmaking, cinematography, film theory
- **Theater & Performance**: Acting, directing, stage design
- **Creative Writing**: Writing techniques, storytelling, poetry
- **Art Criticism**: Art theory, aesthetic philosophy

## Naming Convention

Follow the standard format: `Author_Last_Name - Book_Title.extension`

**Examples:**
- `Gombrich_Ernst - The_Story_of_Art.pdf`
- `Norman_Don - The_Design_of_Everyday_Things.epub`
- `Adams_Ansel - The_Camera.pdf`

## Subdirectories

Feel free to create subdirectories for better organization:
- `visual-arts/`
- `music/`
- `design/`
- `photography/`
- etc.
