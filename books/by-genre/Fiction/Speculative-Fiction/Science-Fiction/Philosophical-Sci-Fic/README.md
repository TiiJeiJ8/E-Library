# Philosophical-Sci-Fic

[EN English](./README.md) | [ZH 中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

This folder collects philosophical science fiction — works that foreground philosophical questions, existential themes, and speculative thought experiments about consciousness, meaning, and human nature.

## Definition & Scope

Philosophical science fiction focuses on ideas and thought experiments. Works included here typically:

- Examine questions about consciousness, identity, free will, ethics, or the nature of reality using speculative or scientific premises.
- Use narrative as a vehicle for philosophical exploration rather than solely for plot-driven action.
- May blend literary and speculative techniques to provoke reflection rather than provide technical explanations.

## Inclusion Criteria

- The work should centrally engage with philosophical themes or thought experiments.
- Scientific or speculative elements should serve to illuminate philosophical inquiry.
- Purely action-driven or plot-focused works without substantive philosophical inquiry should be placed elsewhere.
