# Science Fiction

[EN English](./README.md) | [CN 中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

This folder contains novels centered on scientific progress, technological innovation, and their impact on society and individuals.
The content covers various science fiction elements, including future societies, extraterrestrial life, time travel, and more.

## Definition & Scope
Science fiction is a genre of fiction that emphasizes **science and technology** as core elements.
It typically explores futuristic scenarios, extraterrestrial life, time travel, and their effects on society and individuals.
The genre spans a wide range of themes, from **hard science fiction** (focused on scientific accuracy) to **soft science fiction** (emphasizing social and psychological impacts).

## Inclusion Criteria
- The work should primarily be a fictional narrative.
- The work should feature **science or technology as a core element**, exploring its impact on society or individuals.
- Works may encompass various subgenres, including hard science fiction, soft science fiction, cyberpunk, etc.

## Subgenres

- **Hard Science Fiction** - [`./Hard-Sci-Fic/`](./Hard-Sci-Fic/README.md)
  Speculative works grounded in rigorous scientific principles, emphasizing **scientific accuracy** and **technical detail**.
  Representative works include *Foundation* and *The Martian*.

- **Philosophical Science Fiction** - [`./Philosophical-Sci-Fic/`](./Philosophical-Sci-Fic/README.md)
  Focuses on **philosophical questions, existential themes, and thought experiments** concerning consciousness, meaning, and humanity.
  Representative works include *2001: A Space Odyssey* and *The Three-Body Problem*.
