# Hard Sci-Fic

[EN English](./README.md) | [ZH 中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

This folder contains hard science fiction — works that emphasize scientific accuracy, technical detail, and plausible extrapolation from current scientific knowledge.

## Definition & Scope

Hard science fiction (hard SF) focuses on scientific realism and internal consistency. Works included here typically:

- Base major plot elements on established scientific principles.
- Explore the technical, social, or ethical consequences of scientific or engineering developments.
- Avoid unexamined supernatural or purely fantastical devices unless given a physical or technological rationale.

## Inclusion Criteria

- The work should prioritize plausible scientific foundations or provide detailed technical justification for speculative elements.
- The story should engage substantively with scientific or engineering problems rather than using them merely as background.
- Non-fiction, popular science, or purely speculative fantasy should be placed in other, more appropriate folders.
