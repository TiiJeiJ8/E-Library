# Dystopian Fiction

[EN English](./README.md) | [CN 中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

This folder contains works of **dystopian fiction**—fictional narratives depicting oppressive, totalitarian, or heavily surveilled societies, often as a critique of real-world social systems and power structures.

## Definition & Scope
Dystopian fiction is a genre of fiction that centers on **oppressive or totalitarian societies**, usually set in the future or in alternate realities.
These works construct extreme social environments to explore issues such as **abuse of power, restricted personal freedom, and social injustice**.
Dystopian fiction frequently serves as a critique and reflection on contemporary society, emphasizing skepticism toward political structures and social systems.

## Inclusion Criteria
- The work should primarily be a fictional narrative.
- The work should depict an **oppressive, totalitarian, or heavily monitored society**.
- The work should explore themes of **power abuse, restricted personal freedom, or social injustice**.
- The work should carry a **critical perspective on social institutions and power structures**.
