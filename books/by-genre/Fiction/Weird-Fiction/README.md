# Weird Fiction

[EN English](./README.md) | [CN 中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

Weird fiction is a genre that blends elements of horror, fantasy, and the supernatural to create a sense of the uncanny and unsettling. It often explores themes of cosmic horror, the unknown, and the limits of human understanding.

## Definition & Scope
Weird fiction typically features bizarre, otherworldly, or supernatural elements that defy conventional explanation. It often evokes a sense of dread and unease, challenging readers' perceptions of reality.

## Inclusion Criteria
- Works should incorporate elements of horror, fantasy, or the supernatural.
- Should evoke a sense of the uncanny or unsettling.
- Can explore themes of cosmic horror, the unknown, or the limits of human understanding.
- Can include short stories, novellas, or novels.
