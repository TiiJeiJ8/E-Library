# Fiction 📖

This folder contains fictional works including novels, short stories, and literary fiction.

## Topics Include:

- **Classic Literature**: Timeless novels from renowned authors
- **Contemporary Fiction**: Modern literary works
- **Science Fiction**: Futuristic and speculative fiction
- **Fantasy**: Magic, mythical worlds, epic adventures
- **Mystery & Thriller**: Detective stories, suspense novels
- **Romance**: Love stories and romantic fiction
- **Historical Fiction**: Stories set in historical periods
- **Horror**: Scary and supernatural stories
- **Short Stories**: Collections and anthologies

## Naming Convention

Follow the standard format: `Author_Last_Name - Book_Title.extension`

**Examples:**
- `Orwell_George - 1984.epub`
- `Tolkien_J.R.R. - The_Lord_of_the_Rings.pdf`
- `Christie_Agatha - Murder_on_the_Orient_Express.mobi`

## Subdirectories

Feel free to create subdirectories for better organization:
- `science-fiction/`
- `fantasy/`
- `mystery/`
- `classics/`
- etc.
