# Evolutionary Science Fiction

[EN English](./README.md) | [CN 中文](./README.zh-CN.md)

## Definition and Scope

**Evolutionary Science Fiction** focuses on the **origin, evolution, and future forms of life, intelligence, and civilization**.
It explores scientific principles of evolution and extends them to a cosmic scale, examining how life develops, adapts, and transcends across different environments, technologies, and states of consciousness.
Such works often feature vast temporal and spatial scales, blending **astrobiology, artificial intelligence, cosmology, and philosophical speculation**.

## Inclusion Criteria

- The work should primarily be a fictional narrative.
- It should center on **biological, intellectual, or civilizational evolution**, addressing the origin, adaptation, and transformation of life.
- It should incorporate scientific theories and speculative frameworks to construct fictional evolutionary scenarios.
- It may span from **microscopic biological evolution** to **macroscopic cosmic or civilizational evolution**.
