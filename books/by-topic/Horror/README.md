## Definition and Scope
Horror is a genre that seeks to evoke feelings of fear, dread, and shock in readers. It often explores themes of the supernatural, the macabre, and the unknown, aiming to unsettle and provoke emotional responses.

## Inclusion Criteria
- Works should primarily aim to evoke fear or suspense.
- Can include elements of the supernatural, psychological horror, or the grotesque.
- Can be in the form of novels, short stories, or anthologies.
- Can explore a variety of themes, including but not limited to monsters, ghosts, psychological terror, and dystopian scenarios.
- Can be suitable for different age groups, but should be clearly categorized based on content maturity.
- Can include cross-genre works that have significant horror elements.
- Can be written in any language, with translations included where available.
- Can include both classic and contemporary works.
