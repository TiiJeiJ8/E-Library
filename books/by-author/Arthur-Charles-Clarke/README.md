## About the Author

Arthur C. Clarke (1917–2008) was a British science fiction writer, inventor, and futurist, best known for his visionary hard science fiction and foresighted ideas about technology. His works combine rigorous scientific detail with philosophical reflection and have had wide influence.

## Writing Themes and Style

-- Core themes: the relationship between humanity and technology, space exploration, extraterrestrial civilizations, futurism
-- Style: clear, scientifically grounded, and philosophically resonant
-- Typical audience: science fiction readers and those interested in technology and cosmic themes
