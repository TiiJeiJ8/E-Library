# Non-Fiction 📰

This folder contains non-fiction works including biographies, history, self-help, and more.

## Topics Include:

- **Biography & Memoir**: Life stories of notable individuals
- **History**: Historical events, civilizations, wars, cultures
- **Self-Help & Personal Development**: Productivity, habits, motivation
- **Philosophy**: Philosophical texts and discussions
- **Psychology**: Human behavior, mental health, cognitive science
- **True Crime**: Criminal cases and investigations
- **Essays & Commentary**: Thought-provoking essays and opinion pieces
- **Travel & Adventure**: Travel memoirs and adventure stories
- **Current Affairs**: Politics, society, journalism

## Naming Convention

Follow the standard format: `Author_Last_Name - Book_Title.extension`

**Examples:**
- `Harari_Yuval - Sapiens.pdf`
- `Frankl_Viktor - Mans_Search_for_Meaning.epub`
- `Isaacson_Walter - Steve_Jobs.pdf`

## Subdirectories

Feel free to create subdirectories for better organization:
- `biography/`
- `history/`
- `self-help/`
- `philosophy/`
- etc.
