# Science 🔬

This folder contains books about natural sciences, mathematics, and scientific research.

## Topics Include:

- **Physics**: Classical mechanics, quantum physics, relativity, particle physics
- **Chemistry**: Organic, inorganic, physical chemistry, biochemistry
- **Biology**: Molecular biology, genetics, evolution, ecology
- **Mathematics**: Calculus, algebra, statistics, discrete math, number theory
- **Astronomy & Cosmology**: Space, stars, planets, universe
- **Earth Sciences**: Geology, meteorology, environmental science
- **Medicine & Health**: Anatomy, physiology, medical sciences
- **Scientific Method**: Research methodology, scientific thinking
- **Popular Science**: Science for general audiences

## Naming Convention

Follow the standard format: `Author_Last_Name - Book_Title.extension`

**Examples:**
- `Hawking_Stephen - A_Brief_History_of_Time.pdf`
- `Feynman_Richard - The_Feynman_Lectures_on_Physics.epub`
- `Dawkins_Richard - The_Selfish_Gene.pdf`

## Subdirectories

Feel free to create subdirectories for better organization:

- etc.
