# Business 💼

This folder contains books about business, management, entrepreneurship, and economics.

## Topics Include:

- **Management & Leadership**: Team management, organizational behavior
- **Entrepreneurship**: Startups, business models, scaling companies
- **Finance & Investing**: Personal finance, stocks, financial planning
- **Marketing & Sales**: Digital marketing, branding, sales strategies
- **Economics**: Microeconomics, macroeconomics, economic theory
- **Strategy**: Business strategy, competitive advantage, planning
- **Innovation**: Disruptive innovation, creative thinking
- **Career Development**: Professional growth, workplace skills
- **Productivity**: Time management, efficiency, workflows

## Naming Convention

Follow the standard format: `Author_Last_Name - Book_Title.extension`

**Examples:**
- `Ries_Eric - The_Lean_Startup.pdf`
- `Collins_Jim - Good_to_Great.epub`
- `Thiel_Peter - Zero_to_One.pdf`

## Subdirectories

Feel free to create subdirectories for better organization:
- `entrepreneurship/`
- `management/`
- `finance/`
- `marketing/`
- etc.
